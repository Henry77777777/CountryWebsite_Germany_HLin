Article One Human dignity should never be dishonored. All state authority must respect and protect it. Human rights are binding law. Discrimination based a person, gender, race, disabilty, religion, or origin is illegal. Article Four Freedom of belief, conscience, and religion is guaranteed. No one may be forced into military service against their will. Article Five Everyone may express opinions freely and access information. Freedom of the press and art is guaranteed.
